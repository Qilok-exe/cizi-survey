Cizi survey

These are the results of a short, anonymous survey about how students bank. Most of the people who answered are Boston College students. We ran it on Google Forms for the Citizens Bank case competition. If you have any questions, reach us at qilok.exe@gmail.com.

How we did this

Who
A team of students at Boston College. An independent student project, not run by Citizens or Boston College.
When
October 22 to October 26, 2026.
How
An anonymous Google Form. No names or emails were collected.
Who answered
people, mostly Boston College students ( first-years).

It’s not a random sample, so the results describe the people who answered, not all students. That’s why there’s no margin of error.

Questions appear as asked. We grouped the short answers for two questions (main bank, biggest annoyance). Percentages are rounded, so some won’t add to 100.
